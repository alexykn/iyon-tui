Ode to the Muppet

Soft paws padding on the floor,
A quiet meow at the door.
Whiskers twitch, eyes aglow,
A mystery that steals the show.

By day you sleep in sunlit beams,
Chasing nothing — lost in dreams.
By night you roam with silent grace,
A shadow through the darkest space.

Balls of yarn and boxes bare,
The smallest things become your lair.
You purr a rumble, deep and low,
A gentle hum that says "hello."

Independent, wild, yet tame,
You cannot be tamed or blamed.
You rule the house, you own the chair,
Yet fill our hearts beyond compare.

So here's to you, majestic friend,
On whom our love will never end.
With every pounce and every nap,
You curl the world right in your lap.
